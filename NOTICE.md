# Notice, Authorship and Provenance

## Upstream

**VICE 3.1** is the work of the VICE development team and its contributors.

Future Retro Fusion does not claim authorship or ownership of the upstream VICE
project. Existing copyright notices and licence headers are intentionally
retained.

## Future Retro Fusion

Future Retro Fusion (FRF) maintains the AmigaOS/PiStorm-oriented modifications
present in this repository.

FRF copyright applies only to original FRF-authored modifications,
documentation and integration work, not to unchanged upstream VICE code.

The detailed FRF contribution record is maintained in
[`FRF_CONTRIBUTIONS.md`](FRF_CONTRIBUTIONS.md).

## Distribution

This public source repository intentionally excludes proprietary Commodore ROM
images and user software/media.

Modified VICE binaries distributed from this source should be accompanied by,
or clearly linked to, the corresponding modified source required by the
applicable GNU GPL terms.
